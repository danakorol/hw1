How I got here

My path into technology started in the Israeli Air Force, where I served as a Hermes 450 UAV simulator instructor. From there, I moved on to work as a Sales Demonstrations Manager at Elbit Systems.

Working closely with complex systems made me realize how much I want to build and understand them from the ground up, which is why pursuing my degree in Industrial Engineering and Management felt like the natural next step to advance my career in this field.
